⚠ About these figures
All wealth figures are estimates based on public information (Forbes, Sunday Times Rich List, press reports) at the time of publication. They have not been independently verified and are provided purely for entertainment and perspective.

These figures are manually updated periodically and do not update automatically. Actual wealth may differ significantly — particularly for those whose wealth is tied to share prices, which can change daily.

Historical figures are inflation-adjusted estimates and are not intended to be precise.

The Dollar Book is free to use and run informally on a not-for-profit basis. Any future support goes toward keeping it accurate and online.

Net Zero and climate transition costs vary enormously between sources and methodologies — credible institutions have produced estimates for the same country differing by a factor of ten or more. Figures here are best-available estimates for comparison and perspective, not precise forecasts.

Carbon Offset Share is a deliberately illustrative calculation, not a real allocation or financial mechanism. It simply divides the total value of the world's compliance carbon markets in proportion to each country's Net Zero investment need, to give a sense of scale — this is not how carbon markets actually distribute funds.
Not financial, investment, or legal advice.
