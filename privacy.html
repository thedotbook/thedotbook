Privacy Policy & Data Corrections

Last updated: August 2026

Request a Correction or Removal

The Dollar Book lists publicly estimated wealth figures for public figures, drawn from sources like Forbes and press reporting. If you are featured on the site — or represent someone who is — and believe an entry is inaccurate, out of date, or you would like it removed, we will action this promptly.

  1. Email privacy@thedollarbook.com with the subject line "Correction Request" or "Removal Request".
  2. Tell us the name of the entry and, if a correction, what the correct information should be along with a source if you have one.
  3. We aim to review and action all requests within 5 working days.

What This Site Is

The Dollar Book is a free, not-for-profit, informally run educational and entertainment resource that visualises wealth on a page-number scale (every $ sign represents $1 million). It is not a financial, journalistic, or investigative publication, and none of the figures shown should be relied upon as fact — see the "About these figures" notice on the main site for full detail on sourcing and limitations.

Information We Collect

The Dollar Book is designed to collect as little personal data as reasonably possible:

  • No account or sign-up is required to browse, search, or use the site.
  • "My Own Entries" and "My List" are saved using your browser's local storage, directly on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us, is not visible to anyone else, and stays on your device until you clear it or your browser storage is cleared.
  • "Ask The Dollar Book" (AI feature) sends your typed question, and the name of whichever entry you've selected for context, to our AI service provider in order to generate a response. We do not attach your name, location, or any other identifying information to these requests beyond what you choose to type.
  • Standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be recorded temporarily by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and network provider (Cloudflare) for security and performance purposes, in line with their own privacy policies.

We do not run advertising, do not sell data, and do not use third-party tracking or analytics cookies beyond what is strictly necessary to serve the site.

Information About Featured Public Figures

Wealth estimates shown for public figures are compiled from publicly available sources (Forbes, Sunday Times Rich List, press reporting) and are not obtained from, or verified with, the individuals themselves. This is editorial commentary and perspective-setting, comparable to how such figures are reported in mainstream media, and is presented for entertainment and educational purposes only — not as a statement of fact about any individual's actual financial position.

Your Rights (UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018)

If you are a UK or EU resident, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request erasure of personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how your personal data is processed

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@thedollarbook.com. As the site does not require accounts and stores minimal personal data, most requests can typically be resolved quickly.

Children & Educational Use

The Dollar Book is sometimes used as a classroom teaching aid. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children, does not require any personal information to be entered to use the site, and contains no user-generated content shared publicly between users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "last updated" date above.
